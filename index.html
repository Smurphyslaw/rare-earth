How to play

  1. Every day there are five prompts, the same for everyone, like "Name a country that borders Brazil."
  2. You get one answer and 30 seconds per prompt. Pick a country from the list or press Enter.
  3. Any country that fits scores. The fewer players who give the same answer, the more it's worth.
  4. Stuck? Hint tells you how many countries fit and which continents they're on.
  5. After each answer the globe lights up every country that fits, colored by how rare it was.

Rarity tiers

    Tiers are relative to each prompt. The answer most players give is a Tourist Trap, and the one almost nobody thinks of is Terra Incognita, so every prompt has a gold answer waiting.

    Where rarity comes from

    Streaks, passport and archive

    Play the daily puzzle on its day to build your streak. Every country you name gets stamped in your passport in its tier color. Missed a day? Past puzzles stay playable in the archive on the home page, but they don't count toward your streak.
